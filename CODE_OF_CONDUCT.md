## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in **LayoutLingua** a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback
* Focusing on what is best for the open-source community and scientific reproducibility

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `@ThanhNguyxnOrg/core`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
